TITLE: Label-Efficient Defect Detection with Masked Patch Pretraining

ABSTRACT
Industrial inspection lines produce many images but few labeled defects. We show that
masked patch pretraining on unlabeled line images cuts the labels needed for a target
accuracy by a factor of four.

1 INTRODUCTION
Supervised detectors need thousands of labeled defects per product type. Collecting them
stalls every new production line for weeks.

2 APPROACH
Our approach, Masked Patch Pretraining (MPP), pretrains a ViT-S encoder by masking 60% of
16x16 patches and reconstructing them in pixel space on 200k unlabeled line images. A
light detection head is then fine-tuned on the few labeled defects.

3 DATA
MVTec AD and VisA, plus 200k unlabeled images from our own inspection line.

4 EVALUATION METRICS
Image-level AUROC and pixel-level PRO score.

5 RESULTS
With 50 labeled defects per class, MPP reaches 97.8 image AUROC on MVTec AD, matching a
supervised baseline trained on 200 labels.

6 LIMITATIONS
Pretraining takes 14 GPU hours per product line; transparent parts remain hard.
